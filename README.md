# Adin Segall

ML engineer for music & audio. MSc in Sound & Music Computing at Queen Mary University of London's Centre for Digital Music (Distinction). I DJ, so most of my projects start as problems I actually have.

**What I've built:**

- [dj-playlist-generation](https://github.com/adinsegall/dj-playlist-generation) — my dissertation, presented as an [ISMIR 2026 Late-Breaking Demo](https://ismir2026program.ismir.net/lbd_663.html): natural language → sequenced DJ sets from your own Rekordbox library. Blinded 12-DJ A/B study of heuristic vs. learned-embedding retrieval, LLM enrichment at 97.2% schema-validation, honest null results. *(code private — ask)*
- [riffusion-lora](https://github.com/adinsegall/riffusion-lora) — LoRA fine-tuning of Riffusion for EDM sample generation, with a UNet ablation study. **[Listen to the outputs →](https://adinsegall.github.io/riffusion-lora/)**
- [dl4am-cvae-bilstm](https://github.com/adinsegall/dl4am-cvae-bilstm) — loop-aware audio generation: a BiLSTM loop-candidacy classifier (0.977 val F1) steering a convolutional VAE's latent space
- [audio-fingerprinting](https://github.com/adinsegall/audio-fingerprinting) — a Shazam-style identifier from scratch: constellation maps + combinatorial hashing, 79.3% top-1 on noisy phone recordings
- [music-artist-ontology](https://github.com/adinsegall/music-artist-ontology) — OWL ontology fusing Wikidata + DBpedia, with SPARQL fusion queries and SWRL reasoning
- [WakeTag](https://github.com/adinsegall/WakeTag) — an iOS alarm clock you can only silence by scanning an NFC tag, with any Apple Music song as the wake-up sound

Earlier, at the University of Miami: JUCE/C++ audio plugins — [A-Simple-Synth](https://github.com/adinsegall/A-Simple-Synth), [Channel-Strip-GPT](https://github.com/adinsegall/Channel-Strip-GPT).
